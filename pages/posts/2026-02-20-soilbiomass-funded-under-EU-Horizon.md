# Great news for IDConsortium!

🌱 The SOILBIOMASS+ project has been officially selected for funding under the Horizon Europe Mission “A Soil Deal for Europe”.

Led by the University of Reading and supported by IDConsortium, SOILBIOMASS+ will empower farmers, cooperatives, and rural innovators to co-create solutions that boost sustainable biomass production while restoring and protecting soil health across Europe.

Among only one project selected out of several submissions for this highly competitive topic, we are proud to be a part of this ambitious mission to build climate-resilient value chains grounded in healthy soils and circular innovation.

💬 "A huge thank you to all our partners for the trust placed in us during the proposal phase. We look forward to launching this exciting journey together!" <br />
Macarena Sanz, Director IDConsortium
