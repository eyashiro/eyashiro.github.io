I'm Dr. Erika Yashiro, an independent life scientist, grant writer, and science communicator. I help research teams and professionals move forward faster, effectively, and with more confidence. Over the last two decades, I gradually transitioned from field and lab work to bioinformatics, operational bioinformatics, expert editing, and science communication. Thanks to this broad range of hands-on experience and expertise, I'm able to see the bigger picture of projects and connect the dots. It also allows me to deliver results under real-world constraints: tight timelines, shifting staff, and complex datasets and research infrastructure.

## Why Did I Build Life Sciences Research Support?
#### Every project, team, and timeline is different...
But the bottlenecks are often the same. Maybe you've experienced these too:

* Grant proposals that are all due at the same time.
* Reports, blogs, and web articles that aren't speaking to the stakeholders, the public, or other audiences.
* Manuscripts that need to be clearer, grammar error-free, and more persuasive.
* Unfinished projects.
* Not enough time to handle them all.

My aim is to be a reliable partner who can step in quickly, understand your team's scientific and operational context, and deliver clear results, polished writing, and practical next steps.

#### Private individuals also encounter their own challenges... 
Maybe you've experienced some of these too:

* Job searching or making a career pivot without knowing how to optimise your online presence.
* Not sure how to communicate your expertise to future employers and people visiting your website or LinkedIn page.
* Feeling lost, confused, and alone during thesis, fellowship, or scholarship preparation.

My aim is to help you avoid the mistakes I made and offer practical advice on how to communicate your expertise, decide what to focus on, and confidently take your next step.


## Where Do I Add The Most Value?

* **Grant writing:** I help time-constrained scientists, innovation professionals, SMEs, nonprofits, and novices write clear, compelling grant proposals. Increasing the quality and quantity of applications through our collaboration increases your chances of obtaining grants.
* **Scientific/technical writing:** I help you draft documents, reports, manuscripts, and user guides in clear, precise English. Technical documents often take time to write, so delegating them helps you prioritise your time for other tasks without sacrificing quality or missing deadlines and milestones.
* **Science translation:** I translate your formal science into stakeholder/audience-targeted narratives that speak to your readers. Your online biographies, blog/web articles, and reports will finally make sense to your readers.
* **Rapid, reliable help:** I step in when you need extra staff or when deadlines loom, and finish research, technical, or administrative projects/tasks to keep your stakeholders happy.
* **Advisory support:** I help you understand how to communicate your expertise more clearly on LinkedIn. I also help strengthen your thesis, scholarship, and fellowship applications through practical guidance and critical feedback.


## How Will We Work Together?

#### When there are deliverables
1. **Discovery:** We clarify goals, scope, timeline, rates, and confidentiality requirements.
2. **Proposal:** You get a clear plan with deliverables and milestones.
3. **Communication**: We regularly communicate updates and discuss the work in progress.
4. **Delivery:** You receive deliverables in a timely fashion accompanied by a detailed report (when relevant).
5. **Follow-through:** Edits, revisions, and final polish as needed.

#### For consultation-based services
1. **Discovery:** We clarify goals, scope, timeline, rates, and confidentiality requirements.
2. **Meetings:** Consultation meetings will be pre-scheduled and pre-paid sessions of 60-90 minutes.
