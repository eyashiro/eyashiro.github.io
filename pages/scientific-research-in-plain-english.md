### An initiative that allows students and the community to read fact-checked scientific papers *in plain English* and without AI-generated misinterpretations
<br />

When high school and university students begin to try reading STEM research papers, most of the content passes over their heads. This is because research scientists write their papers assuming that their readers are already experts within their fields of expertise.

The thing is, when it comes to becoming experts in a STEM field, all scientists have to start at the bottom of the ladder. By reading research papers, they become more proficient at understanding them.

However, the initial phase of becoming proficient at reading technical papers is like the lag phase during bacterial growth in liquid culture. It's slow, challenging, extremely frustrating, and a huge deterrent for many who may otherwise have become really successful scientific professionals.

I've started an initiative to build a repository of transcribed research papers in simple English and make them available to the community of budding scientists.

These aren't your typical science summaries you see in most blog posts. They're your research papers transcribed into simpler English that people outside your field of expertise can read and appreciate.

### How it works:
* You're the owner of your story. The transcribed article follows your flow of reasoning, paragraph by paragraph. Therefore, readers will be following the line of thought of the original authors of the research study.

* The complex concepts are simplified and transcribed into plain English, while key terminologies and methodologies are explained. In this way, students can grasp all the main concepts and ideas of the original paper.  Afterward, reading the original research paper should be less challenging because they already understand three quarters of the study.

* Unlike AI-generated summaries, every sentence is fact-checked so that the intended meaning of the authors is retained. (More on AI issues <a href="https://medium.com/@eyashiro/read-scientific-research-papers-without-fear-d9ed93572e34">here</a>.)

### Benefits to you and the community:
* Your research paper will help younger students interested in STEM subjects and introduce them to elite research papers.

* The goal is to build a resource that school teachers and college instructors can use to introduce students to primary literature.

* Your research study in simplified English will reach a much broader community of scientists, students, and science enthusiasts. This is because your transcribed article will be easier to understand, and people will appreciate your work more.

* You can even incorporate the articles from this repository in your own teaching curriculum.
